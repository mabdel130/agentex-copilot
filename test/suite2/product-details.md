# Spec: Product details rendering

Target: https://automationexercise.com/products
Type: product details — read-only

## Acceptance criteria
- A `View Product` action is visible in the catalog.
- The details page displays the product name, category, price, and availability.
- The details page has an Add to cart control without adding an item.

## Scenario
1. Open the Products page, open the first available product detail page, and verify the visible product details and Add to cart control.

## Notes
- Do not add products to the cart. Capture a screenshot and flag console or failed-network errors.
